Epsilon IV

Lead Programmer

2025 · Team of 4 · 5 weeks

  • Epsilon IV was created for the Player2 AI Game Jam #3 in October–November 2025.
  • We used an LLM to power natural language conversation with NPCs, which was required to solve puzzles.
  • Play on itch.io.

“Oh yeah, it's all comin together”

  • On day one we created a game design doc which became the bible for our game.
  • There was zero feature creep.
  • Daily check-ins kept the vision aligned.

“You can't sneak away from the aliens once they hear you. So I just run every time.”

  • Feedback from playtesters indicated our stealth gameplay was broken.
  • We had no dedicated Game Designer to identify problems early on.
  • Our over-ambitious design meant we had little time for revisions.

“Despite the problems…”

  • Epsilon IV won “Best Web Game” and $1000 cash.
  • Average time spent in game is over ten minutes.
  • We learned lessons about scope and the value of a dedicated Game Designer.
